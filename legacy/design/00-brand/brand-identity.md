# Brand Identity Board

## Purpose

Establish the recognizable Paldora wordmark, compact mark, palette, typography pairing, motifs, and photography/material direction before screen generation.

## Required content

- `Paldora` editorial wordmark and compact geometric P/open-box mark.
- Wordmark + sparkle and wordmark + open-box variations.
- Dark and light lockups, monochrome mark, app icon, and favicon-scale test.
- Core palette swatches and the line “a box for your people.”
- Restrained paper, photo-corner, tape, and four-point-star motif examples.

## Constraints

- The wordmark must remain legible at practical sizes.
- No literal treasure chest, ornate crest, excessive constellation, metallic 3D, or juvenile magic aesthetic.
- Small mark must have a clean silhouette and work in one color.
